HOW TO READ IT
  1. 01

    Audio becomes a spectrogram. The first two seconds are resampled to 16 kHz and represented as 128 log-mel frequency bands over 63 frames.

  2. 02

    The CNN compares learned patterns. It returns one sigmoid score for the spoof class; the authentic score is its complement.

  3. 03

    Review the signal views. Use playback, waveform, and spectrogram together. This model does not mark exact frames or identify a voice generator.

Scores are experimental outputs. They are not validated confidence estimates or proof that a recording is genuine or synthetic.
